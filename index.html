the content creator era ✦

chapter 01: it started with a blog post. not a pitch deck, not a whitepaper — just words on a screen and a weird feeling that web3 needed better stories. so i started writing them.

chapter 02: from discord servers at 3am to DAO governance calls on a tuesday morning — i became the person who could translate complex onchain mechanics into content that actually made people care. threads, articles, campaigns, community playbooks.

chapter 03: now? i'm the bridge between builders and their audience. content strategy that doesn't feel like marketing. community architecture that doesn't feel forced. writing that hits different because it's real.
